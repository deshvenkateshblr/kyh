About KYH — what it is and what it is not

A private way to prepare for your doctor

KYH turns your symptoms, routine and diet into questions worth asking. Your answers stay in your browser.

What it is

A preparation tool across Diagnostics, Daily Routine and Diet, built to work alongside a qualified healthcare professional.

What it is not

Not medical advice or a diagnosis. Treat every result as a list of questions for your doctor.

Not for pregnancy, recent surgery or hospitalization, food allergies, or new or worsening symptoms such as chest pain, fainting, blood in the stool or an unexplained lump. If any apply, please see your care team first.

Privacy

Your answers never leave your browser and nothing about you is stored on a server. Only the learning videos come from YouTube, so YouTube can see which videos are shown.

Where the medical logic comes from

The logic is checked against the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements for verified deficiency symptoms, The Merck Manual and Linus Pauling Institute for nutritional research, ACSM & APTA for movement guidelines, and Mayo Clinic for foundational lifestyle protocols.
